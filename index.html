Installing

  1. Tap Download for Android above and open spotmap.apk when it finishes.
  2. Android asks to allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser the first time — allow it, then tap Install.
  3. If Play Protect shows a warning, choose “Install anyway”. That's normal for apps outside the Play Store.

Updates: the app tells you when a new version is out and brings you back here. Downloading again upgrades in place.
