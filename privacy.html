SpotMap Privacy Policy

Effective 23 July 2026

SpotMap is a social map app that lets you see where your friends are and what plans are happening near you. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have — including how to delete everything.

Who is responsible for your data. SpotMap is operated by an individual developer ("we"). For any privacy question, or to request access to or deletion of your data, contact lilcaesar23@gmail.com.

Data we collect

  • Account & identity — your email address and name from the sign-in provider you choose (Google or Apple), plus the nickname and profile photo you set in the app.
  • Phone number — optional. If you add one, it lets friends find you by number and helps match you to their contacts. You can remove it at any time.
  • Precise location — your device's GPS position, and (only if you enable sharing) direction, speed and battery level. See the dedicated section below.
  • Contacts — only if you use "find friends from contacts". Phone numbers are normalized on your device and sent to our server solely to look up which numbers already belong to SpotMap users. They are used for that lookup and are not stored; your address book is never uploaded or shared.
  • Calendar — only if you grant it. Used to add plans you join to your device calendar so you get reminders. We do not read your existing calendar events.
  • Photos — images you pick for your profile avatar or a plan's cover.
  • Plans & social graph — the events you create or RSVP to, and your friend relationships and requests.
  • Push notification token — a device identifier from Expo/Google used only to deliver notifications (a friend's plan, a new RSVP, event reminders).

Location, including in the background

SpotMap collects precise location to place you on the map and, when you turn on sharing, to show your live position to friends you've accepted. With your permission it may collect location in the background — while the app is closed or not in use — so that your position keeps updating for friends during an event.

  • Background collection only happens after you explicitly grant "Allow all the time" and turn sharing on. It never starts silently.
  • Your live location is visible only to friends you have accepted — never to the public or to strangers.
  • You can stop sharing at any time in the app, or revoke location permission in your device settings. When you stop, your position stops updating.

How we use your data

  • To provide the core map, plans, friends and notification features.
  • To match you with friends by phone number or contacts, when you ask us to.
  • To send notifications you've opted into.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or third-party tracking.

Legal bases (EU/EEA users)

Where the GDPR applies, we process your data to perform our contract with you (providing the app), on the basis of your consent (location, contacts, calendar, notifications — each of which you can withdraw), and for our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

Who your data is shared with

  • Other users — your profile (nickname, photo), your plans, and, when you enable it, your live location are visible to friends you accept.
  • Service providers that run the app on our behalf: Supabase (database, authentication and storage hosting), Google (sign-in and Maps), Apple (sign-in), and Expo (push notification delivery). They process data only to provide these services.

Data retention

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account (see below), your profile, plans, friendships and photos are permanently removed. Your live location is not archived — only your most recent position is stored, and it is overwritten as you move.

Your rights & deleting your account

You can access or update your profile in the app at any time. You may delete your account and all associated data in two ways:

  • In the app: open Profile → Delete account and confirm. This immediately and permanently deletes your profile, plans, friendships, photos and account.
  • By email: write to lilcaesar23@gmail.com from your account email and we will delete your data.

EU/EEA and UK users also have the right to access, correct, port, or restrict processing of their data, and to lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.

Children

SpotMap is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from them.

Security

Data is stored with our hosting provider under access controls, and each user can only read the data our rules permit (for example, live location only for accepted friends). No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or requests: lilcaesar23@gmail.com.
